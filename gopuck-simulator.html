HOW TO PLAY

  • 1
    You are Red:

    Sling all pucks counter-clockwise to conquer the next zone.

  • 2
    Avoid Clockwise:

    Going backward triggers a Foul. 3 Fouls = Elimination.

  • 3
    Overrun Knockout:

    If an opponent fills your zone before you clear it, you lose.

  • 4
    Survive to Win:

    Outlast the mechanical chaos to be the last standing.
